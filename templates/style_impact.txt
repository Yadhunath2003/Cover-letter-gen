Tone: Confident but honest — early career, not entry-level apologetic.

The goal is to make one or two moments from your background feel genuinely 
impressive without inflating them. University experience is real experience.

- Open by connecting something specific from the JD to a moment where you 
  did something similar, even in a smaller context.
- Frame your accomplishments with context: what was the situation, what did 
  you actually do, what changed because of it. Even small wins land better 
  with this structure.
- Avoid round fake-sounding numbers. If you don't have a metric, describe 
  the scale or outcome in plain language instead.
- One paragraph should be about why this specific company — not their mission 
  statement, but something concrete about their work, product, or approach 
  that you actually find interesting.
- Don't over-polish. A slightly uneven sentence here and there reads more 
  human than a letter that sounds like it was edited 10 times.
