# Prompts for Impactful Cover Letters

## Opening Hook Prompts
- Start with a specific achievement that demonstrates your value
- Open with a compelling question that relates to the role
- Begin with a brief anecdote showing your passion for the field

## Body Content Prompts
- Highlight 2-3 quantifiable achievements relevant to the position
- Explain how your skills directly solve the company's pain points
- Show knowledge of the company's mission and connect it to your values
- Demonstrate cultural fit by referencing company values or initiatives

## Closing Prompts
- End with a clear call to action and enthusiasm for next steps
- Reiterate your unique value proposition in one sentence
- Express genuine interest in contributing to the company's goals

## Tone & Style Prompts
- Use action verbs and specific examples instead of generic statements
- Keep language professional but personable
- Show personality while maintaining formality
- Vary sentence structure to maintain reader interest

## Key Elements Checklist
- Customize for each role (avoid one-size-fits-all letters)
- Use the company name and specific job title
- Address the hiring manager by name if possible
- Keep to one page maximum
